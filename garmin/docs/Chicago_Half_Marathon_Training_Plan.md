# Chicago Half Marathon Training Plan — Sub-2:00 Goal

**Race:** Life Time Chicago Half Marathon, Sunday, Sep 27, 2026  
**Revised:** Sun, Aug 16, 2026 — 6 weeks out (Week 4 complete)  
**Goal:** Sub-2:00 (9:09/mi average pace)

This revision is based on Garmin data through Aug 16: VO2 max 49.3, half prediction 1:56:24, longest run 7.06 mi @ 10:00 / 165 bpm, easy aerobic load short, high aerobic load over target. Most “easy” runs were actually 9:20–9:40 at 165–172 bpm.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Easy means easy.** Pace 10:15–10:45 **and** average HR **≤155**. If HR is over 155, slow down even if pace slips past 10:45. The Jul 25 treadmill (4 mi @ 10:33, 149 bpm) is the template.
2. **Tempo means tempo.** Continuous miles at **8:50–9:10**. Warm up and cool down easy; do not average 9:20–9:32 and call it threshold. HR in the 165–175 range belongs here, not on easy days.
3. **One hard day per week.** Long run is steady, not a race. Quality day is either tempo or intervals — never both, and never the day after the long.
4. **If you only have two run days,** keep **long + easy**. Do not drop easy to fit speed. Easy aerobic is the current gap.
5. **Bike / swim / gym** only on rest days, moderate, 30–45 min. They do not replace the long run or the quality session.

## Sleep and recovery goals

Garmin sleep need has been ~8–8.5 hours. Actual nights are often ~6h 45m. Friday Aug 15 was score 65 / HRV 45 after a week of too-hard “easy” running.

| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Time in bed | **8 hours** (same window most nights) |
| Time asleep | **≥7 hours** |
| Sleep score | **≥80** (Good) as the default |
| Overnight HRV | Hold the recent **47–51** band; treat a dip to ~45 plus score &lt;70 as a warning |
| Resting HR | Stay near **55**; a jump to 57+ with poor sleep = back off |

**Hard-day sleep rule:** The night before the long run and the night before tempo/intervals, hit 8 hours in bed. If that night’s score is **&lt;70** or HRV is clearly down, **swap the quality session for an easy 4** and do not stack another hard day.

**After a long run:** easy or rest the next day; protect sleep that night (this morning’s 7 miler → tonight still counts).

## Weekly structure

3 runs (Long, Quality, Easy) plus optional gym. At least one full rest or easy cross-training day between runs.

**Gym (30–40 min, easy days only):** squats, walking lunges, single-leg RDLs, glute bridges, planks, calf raises. Moderate. Not a second leg day.

## Week 5 detail (this week)

Space these with a rest/cross day between each. Suggested order: easy → 800s → rest → long → rest.
